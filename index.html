The platform

Two AIs, one safety layer.

Genba Watch sees what's happening on site right now. Genba Sensei knows what every rule, manual, and past incident says about it. Together they close the loop between spotting a risk and fixing it.

Computer vision

Genba Watch

Plugs into the cameras and devices already on your site. No new hardware, no retraining workers — it just starts watching for what matters.

  • Detects missing PPE — helmets, harnesses, vests — in real time
  • Flags people or vehicles entering restricted zones instantly
  • Learns each site's specific layout and risk points over time
  • New: Predicts risk by zone and shift using weather and crew data
  • New: Reads live weather to raise alert sensitivity in bad conditions
Knowledge assistant

Genba Sensei

Every worker can ask a safety question in plain language — in their own language — and get an answer grounded in your actual site manuals and regulations, not a guess.

  • Answers grounded in your site's manuals, MLIT rules, and past reports
  • Speaks and reads back answers in 11 languages, voice or text
  • Every answer cites the exact rule or page it came from
  • New: Workers photo-report near-misses; Sensei learns from every one
  • New: Auto-drafts MLIT-ready incident and compliance reports
